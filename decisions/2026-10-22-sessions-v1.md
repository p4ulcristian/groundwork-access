# sessions v1: signed-in sessions, a step to find them

> **Changed in part** by [Visitors get sessions of their own, and their rows move to the person at sign-in](2026-10-23-visitors-get-sessions-and-their-rows-move-at-sign-in.md) (2026-10-23, v0.7.0): visitor sessions are in; `sign-in!` takes the request and `{:settings :tables :session-flags}`, ends the request's session and moves a visitor's rows; the step adds `:doorman/visitor-id` and `:doorman/session-flags`.

> **Changed in part** by [The session step adds the person to the message's log](2026-10-22-the-session-step-adds-the-person-to-the-log.md) (2026-10-22, v0.3.0): the step also puts the message's log back with `{:person-id …}` added, when someone is signed in.

> **Changed in part** by [doorman is its own library](2026-10-22-doorman-is-its-own-library.md) (2026-10-22): the library is doorman (`doorman.api`); its table is `doorman.sessions`, the person at `:doorman/person-id`; the cookie's name (`groundwork_session` before) and the renewal gap (an hour before) are the required settings `:cookie-name` and `:renew-after-minutes`, and `find-session-key-of-request` takes the settings.

> Builds on [Sessions live in Postgres](2026-10-12-sessions-live-in-postgres.md), [The session is the only truth](2026-10-12-the-session-is-the-only-truth.md) and [doorman ships no logins](2026-10-12-doorman-ships-no-logins.md).

**Decision:**

- `sign-in!` makes a row for a person the app's login has proven and returns the Set-Cookie text: 32 random bytes, HttpOnly, SameSite=Lax, Secure; the table keeps only their SHA-256. `sign-out!` deletes the request's row; `sign-out-everywhere!` every row of a person; `delete-ended-sessions!` is for a nightly background job.
- `make-find-session-step` is the step at the start of a handler: a request that changes something (not a GET, or a websocket) must carry the app's own Origin, or it is answered with an error before any work; then `:groundwork.server.sessions/person-id` goes into the context, nil for nobody.
- A session lasts `:session-days`; each time it is seen its end moves out, at most once an hour.
- Its table is `sessions-table`, in the groundwork folder.

**Why:** the four things every request and every login need, and nothing an app must remember: the Origin check sits in the same step that reads the cookie.

**The price:** visitor sessions (a cart before signing in), embeddable pages and server-to-server routes (decided 2026-10-12) aren't in yet; visitors come next.

**Instead of:** the Origin check as a step of its own: one more to forget to put first.
