# doorman decisions

One file per decision: `YYYY-MM-DD-what-we-decided.md`. The title is the decision. Each file says what we decided, why, and what we chose against. A decision changes by a new file; the old one says so at the top (`Replaced by` or `Changed in part by`), and nothing else in it is rewritten.

The ones dated before 2026-10-22, sessions v1 and the five access pieces were made while doorman was two libraries inside Groundwork, a Clojure framework (groundwork.server.sessions and groundwork.server.access), or before them, in Groundwork's design; they name its parts (its tables, the bells, transport) where they came from. The ones they name that stayed with Groundwork are named by title, in plain text: "Shared rows have an owner", "What every endpoint declares", "A table is one map", "Production migrates before it starts", "Groundwork's own tables are table maps, in their own folder", "Groundwork knows nothing about tenants", "Groundwork is frozen", "The freeze lifts for the access language", "A row can be a pass to one record". Seven moved from Groundwork's own decisions on 2026-10-22, "Groundwork" in them became doorman where it meant sessions or access; Groundwork keeps a short copy of those that also said what Groundwork ships, its routes or its sync. Groundwork now names both the family of libraries this one belongs to and the composer that puts them together; the decisions that stayed with Groundwork are in the composer's `decisions/`.
